# Social media agent: project spec

**Owner:** Chris · **Builder:** Codex or Claude Code, then a Grok Bot · **Runs on:** Grok Bot · **Status:** idea (questions first)

## 0. Charter

**What:** An agent that runs Chris's social presence for the business he picks. It turns what's already happening (calls, wins, client results, his voice notes) into posts, drafts them in his voice and schedules them once he says yes.

**Why:** On Oct 6 Justin proposed a social-media slot in Chris's four-agent plan, and Chris accepted the plan. He never said which accounts. So this starts with questions, not a build.

**Users:** Chris.

**Done means:** For four weeks in a row, a week of posts is drafted every Monday from real material, in his voice. Chris approves in one pass, and they go out on schedule.

## 1. How it works

1. **Material:**
   - the Sales Coach's game film;
   - the Growth Operator's assets;
   - wins in the business;
   - Chris's voice notes (he dictates everything, so a 2-minute voice note can become five posts).
2. Drafts per platform: hooks, captions, carousels, short-video scripts.
3. Chris approves.
4. Posts are scheduled through the platform or a scheduler. They're never published without a yes.
5. Weekly: what worked, and what to make more of.

## 2. Requirements

| # | Requirement | Must / should |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publishes nothing without Chris's yes | Must |
| 2 | Sounds like Chris | Must |
| 3 | Never uses anything about a client, rep or prospect without permission | Must |
| 4 | No health or efficacy claims for regulated products | Must |
| 5 | Repurposes one voice note into several posts | Should |

## 5. INPUT NEEDED (answer these first)

1. Which business or brand: Chris personally, WeScale, the peptide offer or COG?
2. Which platforms (Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube), and which accounts he runs today.
3. Three accounts he wants to sound like.
4. How often he wants to post.

## 6. Acceptance

- [ ] Four weeks of approved, published posts. Engagement tracked against the month before.

## 7. Build order

1. Answer the questions.
2. Drafts only.
3. Scheduling.
4. Weekly learnings.

## 8. Out of scope

Replying to comments or DMs as Chris without approval. Buying followers or engagement.
