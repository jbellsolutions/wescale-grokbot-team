# Creative and funnel ideation

**Inputs:**
- the last 30 days of call clusters (`/workspace/cog-team/vault/Assets/Backlog.md`)
- the top objections and desires in the prospects' own words
- show rate and close rate by lead source, from the COG reports where available
- the current offer

## Produce (one Google Doc per request, in the Drive folder "GrokBot Drafts")

1. **Angles (5):** each is a belief the prospect already has plus the mechanism that resolves it. Tie each to the calls that show it.
2. **Hooks (10):** first lines for video and static ads, in prospect language. Mark which angle each serves.
3. **Ad concepts (3):** format (UGC, talking head, static, carousel), script or outline, visual direction, and the objection it pre-handles.
4. **Funnel idea (1–2):** the path from ad to booked call, where indoctrination content sits, and what changes vs. today, with the metric it should move (show %, close %, cost per booked call).
5. **Landing-page copy draft:** headline, subhead, mechanism section, proof slots (marked `[PROOF NEEDED: sourced]`), FAQ from the real prospect questions, and the CTA.

## Rules

- Every claim needs a source or a `[VERIFY]` tag.
- Respect ad-platform rules: no income guarantees, no personal attributes ("Are you broke?").
- Nothing is published or launched. Drafts go to Chris for approval.
