# Sales asset builder

## Asset types and what "finished" means

| Asset | Finished draft contains |
|---|---|
| **SOP** | Purpose · trigger · numbered steps · who · time limits · what "done" looks like · common mistakes (from the vault events) |
| **Script** (setter or closer) | Opener · discovery questions · transition · pitch/mechanism · close · objection branches · notes on tone, with 2–3 real lines from top closers |
| **Follow-up sequence** | Each touch: day/time relative to the call, channel, message, goal. SMS under 300 characters |
| **Objection doc** | The objection in prospect words · what's underneath · 2–3 responses · proof to use (sourced) · what not to say |
| **Training** | Lesson goal · example call clips (timestamps) · drill/role-play · quiz of 5 questions · scorecard tie-in |
| **Prospect resource** | Who it's for · the problem · the mechanism · proof · CTA. Hand it to the Pre-Call Concierge for the content library |

## Steps

1. Read the backlog row (`/workspace/cog-team/vault/Assets/Backlog.md`) and its evidence. Pull Chris's existing SOPs and scripts (`knowledge.sops_folder` in `/workspace/cog-team/context/cog.json`) so you don't contradict them.
2. Draft in COG's voice. For indoctrination and mechanism language, use `jeremy-haynes-precall-playbook`.
3. Create the Google Doc in the Drive folder "GrokBot Drafts" (`calls.drafts_folder`), through Connect apps. Create new docs only. The title is `[DRAFT] <asset> — <date>`. It opens with a "Why this exists" box linking the evidence.
4. Update the backlog status to `draft ready` and put the link and a 3-line summary in `/workspace/cog-team/daily/<today>/growth.md` and in the summary to Chris. Chief lists it in the brief for him to **approve / revise / drop**.
5. On revise: change only what he asked for, keep a short change note at the top, and ask again.

## Never

- Invent testimonials, numbers, guarantees or income claims.
- Share the doc beyond Chris.
- Edit a live (non-draft) SOP without his OK.
