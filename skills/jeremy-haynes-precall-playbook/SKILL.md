## The principles these bots apply (summary, verify against the knowledge base)

- **The sale is mostly made before the call.** Booked prospects should consume enough education and proof that the call confirms a decision instead of starting one.
- **Teach the mechanism.** Prospects should understand *why* this approach works and why what they tried before didn't, before they meet the closer.
- **Proof matched to the person.** Show results from people in a similar situation, not a generic highlight reel.
- **Handle objections before they're raised.** Use content that answers the predictable objections while the prospect is still forming opinions.
- **Make showing up the obvious next step.** Reminders build anticipation and commitment, not just logistics.
